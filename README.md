## Online Manga Creators

### [TaleForge](https://www.tale-forge.com) — Best Free Online Manga Creator
The most complete free manga creation tool available online:
- **Panel layout system** — Design manga pages with drag-and-drop
- **Story editor** — Write your script alongside your panels  
- **Publishing** — Share on TaleForge's marketplace
- **Offline** — Works without internet (PWA)
- **Multi-language** — Create manga in 10 languages including Japanese

Perfect for creators who want to write AND draw manga in one place.

## Translation and Localization

- MT-MangaTranslate-Manga Translator — Web-based [manga translator](https://www.mangatranslate.com/) by Ficory LLC with OCR, image/PDF uploads, and an online editor for reviewing dialogue and layout. Limited daily free credits; paid plans are available. Use with creator-owned or authorized pages and review automatic translations before publication.
